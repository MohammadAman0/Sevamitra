Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about SevaMitra

What is SevaMitra?

SevaMitra is a local service platform that connects customers with service professionals in Betul.

How can I book a service?

Click on Book a Service, select your required service and submit your booking details. Our team will contact you for further assistance.

Where are SevaMitra services available?

Currently, SevaMitra operates in Betul District, Madhya Pradesh, subject to worker availability.

What services does SevaMitra offer?

SevaMitra offers assistance with electrician, plumber, home cleaning, geyser repair, appliance repair, car and bike washing, mobile repairing, solar panel services and online services.

What are the service charges?

Service charges depend on the type of work, service requirements and materials required. Please confirm the charges before starting the work.

How soon will a worker arrive?

Worker arrival time depends on availability, customer location and service requirements.

How can I join SevaMitra as a worker?

Visit the Join as a Worker section on our website and submit your registration details. Our team will contact you regarding the next steps.

What payment methods are accepted?

Cash and online payment options are available. Please confirm payment details before making any payment.

How can I contact customer support?

For any service-related questions or assistance, contact SevaMitra at +91 73542 87363.
